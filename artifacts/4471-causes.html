Shared parser reads day-first dates as month-first

379 reports across 11 tenants belong to this cause. It includes app and email reports and booking-v2 form reports. The shared parser reads every date month-first even though these tenants use day-first locales, so day and month values flip. For example, R-41200 says a 7 April reminder went out on 4 July.

booking-v3 hint conflicts with tenant date order

People follow the hint and the form reads a different date. For example, R-41230 says a client came on 1 June instead of 6 January.
